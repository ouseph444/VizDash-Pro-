Profile Setup
requires a fit
Scans χ²(θ) by re-minimising over all other parameters. Errors from the Δχ²=1 crossings are asymmetric and correct for non-linear models — unlike the symmetric σ from the covariance matrix.
Interval readout
Run a profile…

1-D Δχ² profile likelihood — thresholds at Δχ² = 1, 4, 9 give 1σ/2σ/3σ intervals

2-D Δχ² confidence regions — filled contours over the joint parameter plane, best fit marked

Normalised likelihood ℒ(θ) — exp(−Δχ²/2), marginal vs profile

Exclusion / significance map — √Δχ² in σ units with 2σ/3σ/5σ isolines
